# The Mini Golf sample

A complete nine-hole course, built only from Piston's own features. Open it from the Hub (**Open Sample: Mini Golf**) and play with **Play** or the Game tab. If you already have a copy from an older Piston, you'll be offered the new one as a separate project (your copy is never overwritten).

## The holes

| # | Name | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Straight Shot | The basics: tee, hole, flag |
| 2 | The Dogleg | A curved fairway |
| 3 | Sand Alley | A skinny path with sand on both sides (surface zones) |
| 4 | The Windmill | Spinning blades: an Animator rotating solid colliders |
| 5 | Rocky Creek | A winding fairway with rocks to bounce off |
| 6 | Tunnel Vision | Two separate sections joined by a tunnel (Tunnel entities and collision layers) |
| 7 | Ice Rink | A slippery zone with rocks in it |
| 8 | Boost Alley | A boost pad and two sliding gates (animated walls) |
| 9 | Grand Finale | A long S-curve with rocks, a windmill and a sand bunker round the hole |

Each hole is a scene (`scenes/hole1.json` ... `hole9.json`) with a **LevelInfo** (title, par, next hole) on its Par label. The fairway is a Polygon with an *inverse* collider, which keeps the ball inside it.

## How it is put together

- **Ball** (`scripts/Ball.js`): pull back to aim and let go. Its look, its `sink` / `tunnelIn` / `tunnelOut` animations, and its dust puff are **components in the scene** (SpriteRenderer, Animator, ParticleEmitter), so you tune them in the Inspector and Animation panel. The script only decides when to play them.
- **Hole** (`scripts/Hole.js`): when the ball touches it, the ball's sink animation plays, the hole fires three confetti emitters, the win sound plays, and then `LevelFlow.finish` records the strokes and loads the next hole.
- **Rock, Blade, Decor, Tunnel**: small game scripts (`scripts/`). The windmill is two **Blade** entities at right angles, each with a looping `spin` clip; the sliding gates are Walls with a `slide` clip.
- **Sounds** (`assets/sfx/*.wav`): hit, wall, bumper, rock, sand, boost, tunnel, sink, win, cheer. The ball picks the sound from what it touched, scaled by speed, through `Piston.audio`. Repeats are throttled so a rattling ball doesn't machine-gun.
- **Art** (`assets/art/*.png`): pixel art on sprite sheets (flag 4 frames, rocks 3, decor 4, hole 2, tunnel 2). Open any of them in the sprite painter and set the Frame size to see the frames.

## Rebuilding the art, sounds and holes

The art, sounds and hole layouts are generated by scripts you can change and re-run (they need Python and Pillow):

```bash
python3 Tools/minigolf/make_assets.py    # sprites and sound effects
python3 Tools/minigolf/make_course.py    # the nine scenes (add --preview for layout pictures)
```

Everything they produce is ordinary project files, so you can also just open any hole in the editor and move things around. The game renders at 1920 × 1080 (see the project's `resolution`) and scales to fit any window.
