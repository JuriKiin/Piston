# The Neon Warden sample

A one-arena twin-stick shooter built to push Piston's graphics: additive glow, dynamic lights, bloom, trails, hundreds of projectiles and a segmented boss. It is both a game and a test of what the engine still lacks (see `docs/showcase/neon-warden/` in the Piston repository for the design, benchmarks and the engine gap list).

**Play:** on the Hub choose **Open Sample: Neon Warden** (it is copied into your projects folder once, and your copy is never overwritten: a newer sample is offered as a separate project), then press Play. WASD moves, the mouse aims, hold the mouse (or J) to fire, Space or Shift dashes (you are invulnerable while dashing, and a dash is how you cross the boss's shockwave rings). R restarts after a win or loss, Esc returns to the title.

**The fight:** a drone swarm, then four turrets, then THE WARDEN: a head, ten armoured segments and a tail core on a figure-eight. Shooting segments breaks them (each costs the head 2 hit points as well); at two thirds health it adds shockwave rings, at one third a sweeping laser.

## How it is put together

| Piece | Where | Engine features it uses |
|---|---|---|
| Floor, arena glow, camera | `scenes/arena.json`, `scenes/title.json` | `SpriteRenderer` (additive blend), `Animator` (glow pulse), `Camera`, **`PostProcess`** (bloom, ambient light, vignette, grade, chromatic aberration) |
| Player, drones, turrets, boss | `scripts/Player.js`, `Enemies.js`, `Boss.js` | sprite sheets with `SpriteAnimator`, `Light2D`, `TrailRenderer`, `ParticleEmitter`, trigger colliders on collision layers, `Animator` spawn/sink clips |
| Bolts and orbs | `scripts/Projectiles.js` | `SpriteRenderer` additive, `TrailRenderer`, `Light2D`, trigger colliders, **`EntityPool`** (recycled entities) |
| Boss body, lasers, aim lines | `scripts/Boss.js`, `Enemies.js` | **`RopeChain`** (the chain), **`BeamRenderer`** (laser and turret aim line) |
| Effects | `scripts/Fx.js` | 16-frame explosion sheets, textured additive particles with drag and spin, `Animator`-driven shockwave rings |
| Game feel | `scripts/Game.js`, `Director.js` | camera shake, zoom punch, hit-stop and slow-mo through `Piston.gameLoop.timeScale`, post-process fields animated from code |
| HUD | `scripts/Director.js` | `UIElement` + `Text` + `SpriteRenderer`, `Animator` for messages |

Game feel (shake, hit-stop, enemy behaviour) is game code on purpose: the engine only provides generic pieces. See *Rendering effects* for the components.

## Rebuilding the art, sounds and scenes
```bash
python3 Tools/neon-warden/make_art.py      # all sprites and the floor (about 15 s)
python3 Tools/neon-warden/make_audio.py    # sound effects and the two music loops (about 5 s)
python3 Tools/neon-warden/make_scenes.py   # scenes/*.json
python3 Tools/neon-warden/preview.py /tmp  # contact sheets of the art, for a quick look
```
Everything is generated by code (Pillow, no other dependencies, no downloaded assets).
